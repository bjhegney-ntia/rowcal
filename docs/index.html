Minutehand

A Google Sheets add-on that turns rows into Google Calendar events, and shows you every change before it happens.

  • Preview first. See what will be created, updated or deleted, then press Sync.
  • No duplicates. Minutehand remembers which row made which event and updates it in place.
  • Times land where you typed them. Minutehand reads times in your calendar's time zone, not the spreadsheet's hidden one, so nothing ends up three hours off.
  • Real spreadsheets. Formula dates, separate date and time columns, all-day events, any column order.
  • Import a date range of events into the sheet; imported rows stay linked.
  • The smallest ask we could make. It sees only the one spreadsheet it is open in, never your other files. It cannot delete or share your calendars, and it never asks for your contacts, email address or profile.

Plans

Free: 50 event changes a month. Pro: unlimited changes and hourly auto-sync.

Support

Something not mapping, or a bug? Open an issue and you will get a reply.
